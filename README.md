## About me

I like turning ideas into things people can actually use.

My work sits at an unusual intersection of **insurance, automation, AI-assisted development, web products, and creative experiments**. I enjoy solving practical problems, but I also like building things simply because an idea is interesting.

> **Build it. Learn from it. Make it better.**

---

## 🧭 What I build

### 🛡️ Insurance & InsurTech
Tools and workflows around insurance, policy data, quotations, reporting, dashboards, documents, and automation.

### 🤖 AI & Automation
I use AI as a practical building partner to reduce repetitive work and turn ideas into working products faster.

### 🌐 Web Products
Lightweight websites, content systems, dashboards, and interactive experiences with a focus on usability and real-world deployment.

### 🌌 Creative Experiments
Interactive projects around reflection, spirituality, visual storytelling, 2D/3D worlds, and ideas that do not fit neatly into a business category.
